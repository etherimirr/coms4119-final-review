Quiz
Assume you are connecting to a web server using HTTP 1.1 w/
persistent connection.

Calculate the # of RTTs needed to fetch and receive an index.html with
5 embedded jpeg images. Assume each image can fit in one packet.

Answer: 2 RTTs are needed for fetching index.html, and then 1 RTT for
each embedded image. Thus, the total duration is 2 + 5 = 7 RTTs.

                                                      Application Layer: 2-2
Recap: Two Communication Architecture
§ Client-server
  • Server is always on with a fixed address, services many
    clients, do not initiate requests with clients
  • Client is sometimes on, sends requests to the server



§ Peer-to-peer
  • No always-on server at all
  • Hosts can come and go, change addresses, can initiate
    communication with any others


                                                              Application Layer: 2-3
P2P Network #1: Napster
§ Sharing audio (typically MP3 music) files
  • Company founded in May 1999
  • First lawsuit in Sept. 1999
  • 80 million users in 2000
  • Ran out of business due to lawsuits in mid 2001
  • Online music store, merged with Rhapsody from BestBuy in 2011
§ Directory service
  • Napster server maintains the list of music files each client has
  • When a client searches on music title or performer, server returns IP addresses
    of online clients with the file
  • Client requests files directly from other online clients

  • Peer-to-peer file transfer, no load on the server

§ No security
  • Clear-text passwords and other vulnerability

§ Rank clients by bandwidth and response time

§ Limitations?

Gnutella: Pros and Cons
§ Pros
   • Fully decentralized, distribute search cost
   • Processing per node allows powerful search semantics


§ Cons
   • Search scope can get huge
   • Search time can get quite long
   • High overhead as node come and go often

P2P Network #3: KaZaA
§ Created by Dutch company in 2001
§ Smart query flooding
  • Flood query within a small set of nodes (super nodes)
  • Heterogeneous nodes: peer and super nodes
§ Combination of Napster and Gnutella
§ Super node tracks the content of all its children
§ Why super node?
  • Query consolidation: propagating query to sub-node      ordinary peer

  • Stability: nodes with high up-time as super nodes       neighoring relationships

                                                                            Application Layer: 2-16
Property #2: Anti Free-Riding
§ Vast majority of users are free-riders
  • Most share no files and answer no queries
  • Other limit the # of connections or upload speed
§ A few peers essentially act as servers

§ Tit-for-Tat
  • Peers decide which peers they upload files to at the high rate
      • Choke other peers, i.e., do not send them chunks
      • Re-evaluate top 4 peers every 10 seconds
  • Occasionally let some free riders download (opportunistic unchoking)
  • newly chosen peer may join top 4
