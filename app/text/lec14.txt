QUIZ: File distribution time
100 clients would like to download a popular file (50MB). Each client
has 1Mbps download capacity and 0.5Mbps upload capacity.

§ How much time would it take if they are downloading from a server
  in a client-server architecture, assuming the server supports 10
  concurrent downloads?
   A: 10 * (50M*8/1Mbps) = 4000 secs
§ How much time would it take if they form a BitTorrent P2P system,
  assuming perfect distribution? Approximation is fine.
  A: 50MB*8*100/(0.5Mbps*100) = 800 secs

Two kinds of loss in TCP
§ Triple duplicate ACK
   • Packet n is lost, but packets n+1, n+2, … arrive
   • Receiver sends duplicate ACKs, sender resends packet n
à Do a multiplicative decrease and keep going

§ Timeout
   • Packet n is lost and detected via a timeout (e.g., because all packets in flight
     were lost)
   • After the timeout, blasting away for the entire CWND, would trigger a very
     large burst in traffic
à Better to start over with a low CWND

§ congestion control without inducing/forcing loss
§ control is triggered by increased delay/RTT, not loss events
§ maximizing throughout (“keeping the just pipe full… ”) while
  keeping delay low (“…but not fuller”)

  § detect congestion earlier and reduce cwnd earlier when competing

TCP deployments often implement network-assisted congestion control:
 § two bits in IP header (ToS field) marked by network router to indicate congestion

 § congestion indication carried to destination

 § involves both IP (IP header ECN bit marking) and TCP (TCP header C,E bit marking)
