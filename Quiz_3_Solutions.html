  1. a) how does TCP detect corrupted bits in a received TCP segment?

b) what are the two mechanisms TCP use to detect packet losses? how does TCP change its congestion control window immediately after each type of packet loss detection?

Answer: a) TCP uses checksum field in the TCP header to detect bit corruption.

b) TCP can detect packet loss using either time-out, or triple duplicate ACKs.

After a timeout loss, TCP reduces its congestion window to 1, then do slow-start; after a triple-dup-ACK loss, TCP reduces its congestion window by half, then enter congestion avoidance.
